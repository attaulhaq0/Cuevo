## Current verified facts for architecture

BSO is a voluntary DfE accreditation scheme for overseas schools. Schools must demonstrate that they meet the BSO standards through an inspection by an approved inspectorate. DfE has no jurisdiction over schools operating outside England.

The standards include curriculum, teaching, pupil welfare/health/safety, behaviour, bullying prevention, and other whole-school requirements.

## Qatar note

DfE's accredited-school list includes Qatar schools and marks some country-specific standards as legally constrained by host-country law. Do not assume an England requirement automatically overrides Qatar law.

## No compliance claim

Edeviser may say:
“Evidence mapped to BSO standard X.”

It may not say:
“School is BSO compliant/accredited.”
