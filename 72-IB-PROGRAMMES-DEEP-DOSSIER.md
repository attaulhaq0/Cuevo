## PYP

PYP is inquiry-oriented and transdisciplinary. Do not force it into a conventional subject-only annual syllabus model.

Model where applicable:

- transdisciplinary themes
- programme of inquiry
- subject/domain learning
- learner agency/reflection
- school-developed learning experiences
- feedback-oriented assessment context

The PYP does not use formal summative assessment in the same way as MYP/DP; its public assessment guidance emphasizes feedback, self-assessment and reflection.

## MYP

Model:

- eight subject groups
- subject-group objectives
- criteria
- achievement levels
- interdisciplinary learning
- projects/long-term experiences as applicable
- school-based assessment and moderation workflows where applicable

MYP must not be represented as a percentage-only gradebook.

## DP

Model the DP as:

```text
Six subject groups
+
TOK
+
CAS
+
Extended Essay
```

Course results use a 1–7 representation and the diploma has programme-level requirements beyond course grades.

Source:
https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/

## CP

Model:

- applicable DP courses
- career-related studies
- CP core
- reflective project
- community engagement
- personal and professional skills
- language and cultural studies

Official assessment page:
https://ibo.org/programmes/career-related-programme/assessment-and-exams/

## Assessment rule

IB programmes have materially different assessment systems. The core Edeviser model stores native assessment representation; normalization is only for analytics where a valid transformation exists.

Official assessment overview:
https://ibo.org/about-the-ib/what-it-means-to-be-an-ib-student/recognizing-student-achievement/about-assessment/

## Rights boundary

Use official public structure and authorized school resources. Do not recreate restricted IB educator resources from public descriptions.

## Customer-ready rule

"IB supported" is not valid unless the exact programme(s), version/context, assessment/reporting workflows and required components have been validated.
