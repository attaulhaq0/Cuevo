# Edeviser Product Constitution

## Product

Edeviser is a standalone K–12 Learning Experience Platform.

It includes SIS/MIS and LMS capabilities because a real LXP needs school identity/context and a dependable daily learning environment. Those capabilities are foundation layers, not the whole product identity.

The product differentiator is the connected loop:

**curriculum → learning activity → assessment → evidence → learner state → intelligence → action → measured outcome → improvement.**

## Five experiences

### Student
“What should I learn or do next?”

### Teacher
“Who needs me, why, and what action can I take?”

### Parent
“How is my child progressing and how can I support them?”

### Coordinator
“Where are curriculum, attainment, evidence or intervention gaps?”

### Admin
“How is the school operating and what controls should I configure?”

## Product principles

- learning experience first
- evidence before inference
- explainable before impressive
- human control over consequential decisions
- curriculum-neutral core
- configuration-driven tenancy
- privacy by design
- bilingual/RTL from the start
- social interaction with child safety boundaries
- intelligence over dashboard clutter
- measured intervention rather than recommendation-only AI

## What Edeviser is not

It is not:
- an autonomous grading authority
- a student surveillance system
- a psychological profiling engine
- an AI-only tutor
- a generic social network
- an accreditation guarantee
- a replacement for official curriculum/qualification authorities

## North-star question

For every meaningful learning event, Edeviser should increasingly help a school answer:

**What happened? Why does it matter? What evidence supports that conclusion? What could we do next? Did the action work?**
