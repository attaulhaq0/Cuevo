# Behaviour, Pastoral and Safeguarding Boundary

## Important distinction

Edeviser has a structured school behaviour/pastoral domain because whole-school products need it.

It remains separate from:
- academic attainment
- Habit Engine
- learner development
- AI inference

## MVP

Only basic:
- recognition
- incident record
- teacher/admin notes
- approved follow-up
- audit

Safeguarding cases must not be exposed to general student/parent dashboards.

## AI boundary

AI can help:
- summarize authorized notes
- draft neutral administrative text
- flag duplicate/incomplete record fields

AI must not:
- diagnose
- decide safeguarding outcome
- label a child as dangerous
- infer abuse
- infer mental health
- automatically escalate without a defined policy and human owner

## Access

Highly restricted.

Every read is purpose- and role-limited.

## Retention

Follow school/legal retention policy per jurisdiction.

Do not assume academic retention equals safeguarding retention.
