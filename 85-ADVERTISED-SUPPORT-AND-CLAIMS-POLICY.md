# Advertised Support and Claims Policy

## Principle

Product UI, website and sales material must use the same verified support denominator as the internal coverage matrix.

## Curriculum claims

Do not say:

- "supports Cambridge"
- "supports IB"
- "fully British curriculum"
- "Qatar compliant"

unless the exact claim has passed the documented support criteria.

Prefer:

- "England National Curriculum KS2 Mathematics — validated version/date"
- "Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics 0580 — syllabus version/date"

## Accreditation claims

Do not say:

- "QNSA compliant"
- "BSO compliant"
- "CIS accredited"
- "guarantees accreditation"

Edeviser may say:

- "provides an evidence workspace for [framework]" when the exact framework pack is validated;
- "helps organize evidence and improvement actions" where that functionality is demonstrated.

## AI claims

Do not say:

- "AI predicts student success" without a validated model and defined population;
- "AI detects mental health";
- "AI knows why a student is struggling."

Prefer:

- "Edeviser identifies evidence-backed attention signals."
- "Edeviser Intelligence proposes supported next actions."
- "Teachers retain control over consequential decisions."
