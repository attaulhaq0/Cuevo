# Quality and Accreditation Model

## Principle

Quality/accreditation is a separate axis from curriculum.

Edeviser can organize evidence for a framework without claiming that the school is accredited or compliant.

## Framework model

```text
QualityFramework
 ↓
Version
 ↓
Area
 ↓
Standard
 ↓
Indicator
 ↓
Evidence requirement
 ↓
School evidence
 ↓
Self-study / action
 ↓
Review
```

## Frameworks planned

- Qatar National School Accreditation (QNSA)
- British Schools Overseas (BSO)
- Council of International Schools (CIS)

## Evidence reuse

One school activity can support multiple views:

```text
Teaching activity
 ↓
Evidence
 ├─ curriculum
 ├─ learner improvement
 ├─ CQI
 └─ quality framework
```

## Never

- “You are accredited”
- “Edeviser guarantees compliance”
- “AI verified accreditation”

Instead:
- evidence available
- evidence missing
- review required
- school-entered interpretation
- external decision pending

## Versioning

Framework versions must be immutable.

School reviews retain the framework version used at the time.
