# CIS International Accreditation Framework — Structural Implementation

## Official source

https://www.cois.org/for-schools/international-accreditation

## Current verified structural description

CIS describes its International Accreditation Framework as multidimensional and focused on socially responsible learning communities.

Its current public material describes areas including:
- Purpose and Practices/Planning
- Guiding Principles
- Learning
- Well-being
- global citizenship/social responsibility themes

The framework emphasizes school development and continuous improvement rather than a simple document checklist.
