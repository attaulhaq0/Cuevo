# CQI and Continuous Improvement

## Purpose

CQI turns measured learner outcomes into school-level improvement.

## MVP

MVP has a minimum closed loop:

```text
Problem
 ↓
Intervention
 ↓
Measurement
 ↓
Outcome
```

This is enough to prove improvement logic.

## Post-MVP CQI

```text
Learner
 ↓
Class
 ↓
Subject
 ↓
Department
 ↓
School
 ↓
Improvement objective
 ↓
Action
 ↓
Owner
 ↓
Measure
 ↓
Review cycle
```

## CQI action

Fields:
- objective
- baseline
- evidence
- action
- owner
- start/end
- measures
- status
- review date
- outcome
- reflection

## Intelligence

AI can summarize evidence and suggest improvement actions.

It does not declare school compliance or accreditation.

## Evidence graph relationship

CQI should link to existing evidence rather than require manual duplication.
