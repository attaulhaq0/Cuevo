# Communication

## Channels

- announcements
- teacher/student messages
- teacher/parent messages
- group discussions
- notification center

## Message states

DRAFT
→ SENT
→ DELIVERED
→ READ

Delivery is not guaranteed; show failures.

## Sensitive communication

Sensitive categories require school policy and may require human review.

Do not let AI automatically send high-impact messages.

## AI assistance

Allowed:
- draft
- summarize
- translate
- suggest clearer wording

Approval:
- teacher/staff reviews before sending when configured.

## Notifications

Use:
- in-app
- web push where enabled
- email where configured

Avoid notification overload.

## Safety

- school scope
- role permissions
- rate limits
- audit
- report mechanism
- attachments protected
