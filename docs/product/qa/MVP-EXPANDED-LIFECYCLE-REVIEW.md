## Ruled out and qualified criticism

- Generic assignment/quiz activity completion increments participation, but verified practice/reflection/feedback-linked revision sources govern Habit/XP. It does not create an academic grade or assignment XP.
- Existing native numeric/rubric results, human approve/edit/reject, current source guards and non-causal outcome measurement are substantive. Keep them.
- Disabled student-to-student private chat, optional email/push, every agent mode/tool, broad multi-agent tutoring and every grading model are not automatically Gate5 blockers.
- Announcements do not establish bidirectional parent messaging. Where enabled by approved school policy, the documented teacher/parent channel needs actual implementation; unrestricted minor chat remains excluded.
- Campus/profile/goals/portfolio organization and broader administrative screens require explicit scope tracking. A single-campus reference setup and operator account provisioning do not prove complete SIS/onboarding workflows.
- England selected-subject, Qatar jurisdiction and Cambridge0580 are included targets needing approved source artifacts and technical activation. They cannot be relabeled post-MVP simply because rights are unresolved.
