# Edeviser in Plain English

## What the whole app does

### SIS/MIS
Knows who belongs to the school.

### LMS/LXP
Hosts the learning experience.

### Curriculum Engine
Knows what the school is trying to teach and how its chosen programme describes learning.

### Assessment
Records how learners demonstrate learning.

### Evidence
Stores why a result or conclusion can be supported.

### Learner State
Maintains a longitudinal picture of learning.

### Habit Engine
Records observable learning behaviours such as practice, revision and reflection.

### Intelligence
Interprets authorized evidence and proposes useful next actions.

### Personalization
Chooses an appropriate learning experience for a learner.

### Intervention
Turns an approved recommendation into a concrete action.

### Measurement
Checks whether the action changed the outcome.

### Community
Lets learners and teachers interact safely.

### Gamification
Makes healthy learning behaviors visible and rewarding.

### Communication
Connects school, teachers, learners and parents.

### Portfolio
Shows meaningful learning evidence over time.

### CQI
Turns measured improvement into school-level improvement work.

### Accreditation
Organizes evidence for applicable frameworks.

## Why it is an LXP

The student's primary experience is not administration.

It is:
- what to learn
- what to do next
- understand feedback
- practice
- reflect
- collaborate
- see progress
- get appropriate support
- build a learning journey

The school operating modules exist underneath to make that experience trustworthy and workable at institutional scale.

## Why it can become intelligent

The AI has:
- curriculum context
- assessment context
- evidence
- learner state
- prior interventions
- outcomes

That is much richer than a chatbot with no school context.

## Why it can be modular

A school chooses:
- curriculum
- programme
- modules
- quality frameworks
- policies

The same codebase remains underneath.

## Why it can scale

The core model is curriculum-neutral.
Curriculum content becomes versioned packs.
School differences become configuration.
AI uses a governed orchestration layer.
Critical academic writes remain deterministic.

## What the MVP proves

A single learning improvement cycle works.

Once that is verified, the team can safely expand breadth.
