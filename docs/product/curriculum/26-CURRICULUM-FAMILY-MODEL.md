# Curriculum Family Model

## Principle

A family is a grouping of related educational pathways, not one curriculum.

## British / England family

```text
England
 ├─ EYFS
 ├─ National Curriculum KS1
 ├─ National Curriculum KS2
 ├─ National Curriculum KS3
 ├─ National Curriculum KS4
 └─ Post-16 qualifications (often delivered separately)
```

International qualifications commonly used by British/international schools are separate qualification providers/pathways.

## Cambridge family

```text
Cambridge Pathway
 ├─ Primary
 ├─ Lower Secondary
 ├─ IGCSE
 ├─ O Level
 ├─ AS Level
 └─ A Level
```

These have materially different assessment behavior.

## IB family

```text
IB
 ├─ PYP
 ├─ MYP
 ├─ DP
 └─ CP
```

Do not model all IB programmes as one assessment model.

## Pakistan

```text
Pakistan
 ├─ National Curriculum
 ├─ current/rationalized versions by grade/subject
 ├─ provincial/local pathways where applicable
 └─ international qualifications such as Cambridge
```

Exact curriculum version must be explicit.

## Qatar

Qatar is a jurisdiction context with multiple educational systems in private schools.

```text
Qatar jurisdiction
 +
school curriculum/programme
 +
local requirements
 +
quality/accreditation
```

Do not treat Qatar as one universal curriculum for all private schools.

## Golden architectural rule

```text
Curriculum family
   ↓
Programme
   ↓
Version
   ↓
Adapter/pack
```
