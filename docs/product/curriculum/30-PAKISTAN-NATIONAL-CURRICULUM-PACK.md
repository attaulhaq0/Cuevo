## Critical version rule

Pakistan curriculum must be versioned aggressively.

The NCC publishes current/rationalized material and draft/revised content.

Do not create a permanent “Pakistan 2026” curriculum without recording:
- notification/reference
- publication date
- effective date
- grade
- subject
- version
- status

## National Curriculum model

Support:
- grade/year
- subject
- curriculum reference
- standards/learning outcomes where available
- content areas
- assessment model where authoritative
- school scheme of studies
- local/provincial overlays

## Provincial/local expansion

Pakistan's national framework does not mean every school has exactly the same operational assessment or examination pathway.

Represent local/provincial/exam-board contexts separately.

## International qualification path

Cambridge IGCSE/O Level/AS/A Level may be used by Pakistani schools.

Do not merge Cambridge assessment rules into the Pakistan National Curriculum pack.

## Customer-ready rule

A Pakistan pack is customer-ready only after:
- current source/version verified
- applicable authority identified
- academic reviewer assigned
- assessment/reporting rules verified
- school archetype tested
