# Current Research Checklist — 1 October 2026

## Re-verified facts used by this pack

### England
The official England National Curriculum framework covers key stages 1–4 and includes programmes of study and attainment targets.

### EYFS
A separate EYFS statutory framework applies in England to birth to 5; it is distinct from KS1–KS4.

### Cambridge
Cambridge Primary and Lower Secondary provide distinct programmes with optional/recommended assessment structures.
Cambridge IGCSE is a qualification with subject-specific assessment arrangements and multiple possible assessment components depending on subject.
Cambridge Pathway also includes O Level and AS/A Level contexts.

### IB
PYP, MYP, DP and CP have materially different structures.
DP includes six subject groups and a core; DP course grades use 1–7; internal/external assessment exists.
MYP uses subject-group objectives/criteria and school-based assessment with optional external eAssessment.
CP combines DP courses, CP core and career-related study.

### Pakistan
The National Curriculum Council publishes current/revised curriculum material; rationalized Grade 9–12 curriculum material is available for 2026 in the official source.

### Qatar
The Ministry private-school landscape includes multiple curricula.
QNSA is a national school accreditation system and has been applied to private/international schools.
Qatar is therefore a jurisdiction/configuration layer, not a single curriculum for all private schools.

### BSO
BSO is a voluntary DfE accreditation scheme for overseas schools with defined standards and approved inspection processes.
DfE does not have jurisdiction over schools outside England.

### CIS
CIS International Accreditation is a school-development/evaluation framework involving purpose, learning, well-being and global citizenship dimensions.

### Supabase
RLS and grants are separate security controls.
New tables are no longer automatically exposed by default in the current Data API model.
Realtime protected channels can be authorized with RLS and private channels.
