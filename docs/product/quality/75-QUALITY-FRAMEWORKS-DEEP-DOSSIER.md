# Quality / Accreditation Frameworks — Deep Implementation Dossier

## Principle

Quality/accreditation systems are NOT curricula.

Represent them independently.

```text
Curriculum
Jurisdiction
Quality framework
```

can all apply simultaneously.

## BSO

British Schools Overseas is a standards/inspection framework for British schools overseas, with standards and inspection reports published by the UK Department for Education. The current public reports page was updated in August 2026 and includes Qatar schools among the accredited schools listed.

Official sources:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/british-schools-overseas-standards-for-schools
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/british-schools-overseas-inspection-reports

Implementation concept:

```text
BSO framework
 ↓
standard / requirement
 ↓
school evidence
 ↓
review status
 ↓
action
 ↓
follow-up evidence
```

## CIS

CIS International Accreditation is a school improvement and accreditation framework developed by CIS. Its framework emphasizes strategic alignment and evidence of improvement, while its membership standards include governance, whole-school safeguarding, design for learning and socially responsible leadership.

Official sources:
https://www.cois.org/for-schools/international-accreditation/framework-and-protocol
https://www.cois.org/for-schools/school-membership/membership-standards

Edeviser must represent CIS standards as a quality evidence model, not as curriculum objectives.

## QNSA / Qatar National School Accreditation

The Ministry of Education and Higher Education states that Qatar's National School Accreditation System has applied to private and international schools since 2011 and aims to enhance performance and continuous improvement.

Source:
https://edu.gov.qa/en/News/Details/10669200

The exact active standard set, version, evidence requirements and workflow must be captured from current authoritative material before customer-ready implementation.

## No automatic compliance claims

Edeviser may assist with evidence organization, mapping and CQI workflows.

It must not claim:

- a school is accredited;
- a school is compliant;
- a school will pass inspection;
- Edeviser itself grants accreditation.

Those are external decisions.
