## Review, corrections and remaining issues

Every primary image was visually inspected against its inventory and current source boundary. Targeted correction passes removed invented learner traits, slogans, numeric scores, unsupported live tutoring, raw parent submission access, absent editing/publishing contracts, automatically granted permissions and incorrect grade/recognition semantics. The originals and discarded corrections remain local historical evidence. A reviewed label means the selected concept is suitable for visual discussion; it does not establish user approval, production accessibility or functioning controls.
