## Experience grammar

The visible sequence is **goal → relevant evidence → proposal → human review → approved action → follow-up**. Hide implementation jargon behind deliberate provenance detail. The interface should feel helpful because it connects the current task to context and the next useful step; a mascot, chatbot or gradient alone cannot accomplish that.

- An action-first home answers what needs attention and what is next using current authorized records. A contextual helper lives beside the active task.
- Explain why a suggestion appeared, cite authorized human-labelled sources and show meaningful unknowns. Separate interpretation from teacher/academic records.
- Offer specific intent actions such as review feedback, plan a lesson, inspect evidence, compare follow-up, or open permitted school settings. Do not present a generic unlimited input as permission to access everything.
- Give multi-step progress plain labels such as checking available evidence, preparing a suggestion and ready for review. Do not invent executed work, progress percentages or tool steps without actual state.
- Present the proposed action preview, editable content, reason and consequence before important mutation. Confirm receipts and preserve original uncertain retries.
- After action, show current support/reassessment/outcome context. Observed improvement does not prove AI caused it; missing evidence remains unknown.

## Student buddy: functional scope

Design a recognizable optional companion across the meaningful loop: choose work → open authorized lesson → attempt → use available approved material/feedback → practise/revise → reflect → confirm receipt → see recorded recognition. Explicit appearance, interaction preference and quiet/hide choices personalize the visual experience without inferring traits or granting advantages. Current safe local choices are session-only until durable preference/retention policy exists.

Current APIs support published course/lesson/activity, own submission/draft/revision, teacher return feedback, approved support completion/reflection, released native results and school-approved practice/revision/reflection recognition. A companion can compose these into a useful study session.

Live hints, explanation of an attempt, conversation and durable companion memory need a new student-scoped workflow with purpose, actor, data classification, authorized tools/context, provider/data policy, output schema/citations, audit, failure behavior and budget/latency limits. Do not reuse staff Teacher Insight behind the student UI. Assessment-help policy and private quiz answer-key exclusion need explicit rules. The model never submits work, grades, grants access or creates authoritative curriculum/evidence.

Keep warmth task-related. No emotional dependence, guilt for absence, personality/mental-state labels, invented mastery/streaks or composite child score. Recognition is separate from academic attainment and tied to confirmed approved recorded actions.
