## How to read the atlas

There are four different inventories:

1. **Source37 role screens:** all 59 named screens in [37 UI screen inventory](../product/design/37-UI-SCREEN-INVENTORY.md), including screens whose dedicated implementation is absent.
2. **Current subsection registry:** the actual visible workspace views, tabs, embedded detail sections, forms and disclosures in the isolated application source. A subsection is not necessarily a URL or a standalone screen.
3. **Active approved-auth direction:** 34 prompt-ready full-screen concepts, comprising 33 current-capability-backed compositions and one proposed companion-preference surface. Current describes the underlying supported behavior, not an already implemented layout. Prepared prompts are not generated or accepted images.
4. **Historical comparison grid:** 24 anchors across three former creative directions, a planned scope of up to 72 frames. The old plan and F01–F24 references are retained for traceability. They are not the active generation scope or evidence that 72 images were generated.

The current [shell](../../apps/web/features/shell/components/workspace.tsx) has one root application route and twelve logical views: Overview, School, Community, Portfolio, Development, Curriculum context, Learning, Academic, Progress, Next steps, Access details and Account. Feature tabs and deep controls sit inside those views. The navigation paths below are human review paths, not promised URL paths.

In the tables, **Existing** means source contains the relevant surface; **Embedded** means the named source screen is represented inside another view; **Partial** names a concrete implemented subset and a remaining gap; **Unimplemented** means no dedicated current customer surface was found; **Proposal** means a requested new concept without implementation. Existing is a source observation, not rendered QA. A family frame reference indicates the closest design language to inspect; it never proves that an unimplemented screen exists or that an unrendered subsection was approved.

## Active direction from approved authentication

The selected 1536x1024 auth concept is recorded in the [auth asset decision](../decisions/2026-10-01-auth-presentation-assets.md) and [auth integration plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-01-auth-and-character-integration.md). The approved image and ignored `.local/design-concepts/auth-refinement/auth-senior-concept.png` share SHA-256 `f562e3d57bbeca0db6741fc349172cdc15c8e40759da94e9d59a392317e9cdc5`. Auth uses a full-bleed 62.5% illustrated region and 37.5% white form region, a generated pale blue ribbon background, waving fox and official mark. The workspace inherits its visual grammar rather than repeating the sign-in split or giant heading.

Every active concept is one full screen at **1536x1024**. Use a compact horizontal role header with the official Cuevo logo, current school/role context, language and account, plus contextual navigation/tabs/breadcrumbs beneath it. **No permanent sidebar.** Keep relevant destinations visible through compact role navigation; any drawer/menu is deliberate, not a fabricated global search or school switch. Pearl blue canvas, navy hierarchy and blue/cyan primary actions connect every role. A restrained dimensional ribbon may frame the header; opaque white workplanes keep lessons, tables, forms and evidence readable. Blue learning, teal practice, violet feedback and warm recognition accents reuse one semantic system. Adult screens have compact titles, mature queues and focused evidence/decision panels. Student screens have a generous working canvas, optional supplied fox/owl companion and record-backed learning milestones. The auth-generated fox and supplied ZIP poses remain separately identified in the [character plan](2026-character-mvp-and-growth.md).

The companion guides current work: choose available learning, read teacher material/feedback, attempt, practise/revise, reflect and confirm completion/recognition. It cannot promise a live chat, generated hint, inferred mood, mastery, durable memory or academic advantage. Neutral/reading/recognition poses follow real task state; no worried/failure or guilt expressions. Adults receive helpful contextual actions, with AI origin, uncertainty, evidence and approval visible wherever the existing Teacher Insight workflow is shown. No fictional metrics, grades, streaks, attainment percentages or causal improvement claims. Human headings use authorized record context; missing names/status have plain unknown/recovery states and technical keys stay inside intentionally opened provenance detail.
